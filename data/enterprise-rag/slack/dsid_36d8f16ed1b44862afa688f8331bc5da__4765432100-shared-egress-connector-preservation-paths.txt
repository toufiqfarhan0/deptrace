Matt: Proposed design sketch:
- Connector runs in customer-region VPCs with a small warm ENI pool per AZ.
- When tenant requests preservation, control plane reserves an ENI and programs TGW routes.
- If pool exhausted, connector assigns a stable SNAT IP from a tenant-assigned NAT map.
- Control plane issues short-lived attestation tokens; connector uses those to fetch ephemeral certs from KMS-backed store.
