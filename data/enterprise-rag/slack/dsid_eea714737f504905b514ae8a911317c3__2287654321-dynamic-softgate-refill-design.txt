maria: Queue depth peaked ~2.3k for the us-west A100 pool, sustained 20m. Median wait for small-gen was ~400ms but p95/p99 up to 4s/7s. We saw a handful of large keys burning burst tokens and starving others.

tim: Proposal sketch: abandon purely hard per-key caps. Introduce a ‘‘soft gate’’ layer where refill rates are scaled by regional headroom. Instead of immediate 429 on token miss, attempt short enqueue (<=2s) with ETA metadata; if ETA exceeds threshold, return 429 + Retry-After + fallback recommendation.

maria: Rough refill formula idea:

```
# per-key refill modifier
headroom_ratio = free_slots(region, class) / target_headroom(region, class)
refill_mult = clamp(headroom_ratio, 0.0, 1.0)
refill_rate = base_refill * refill_mult
# per-org scale
org_refill = refill_rate * (org_commitment / total_commitment)
```

liz: Important: headroom must be computed per-region and per-instance-class. Can't mix H100/A100 across pools because models pinned to instance classes.

ajay: Need priority tiers: enterprise keys have a lower bound (min_refill) and a dedicated reserved pool. Internal CI/canary keys get emergency short-lived bypass tokens with strict rate/ttl.
