aj: I'd push for central authorizer. duplication in proxy means policy drift and a heavier rollout surface. proxy check = optimization, not source of truth.
maria: agree on SOT. but proxy gating could save a ton of wasted work for expensive backends (inference / dedupe). tradeoff = duplication + config complexity.

aj: metrics to add: `authz.proxy.evaluations`, `authz.proxy.soft_denies`, `authz.proxy.hard_denies` and a histogram for latency impact. if proxy does cheap reject, measure upstream saved CPU.
