Priya (Benefits): Hi Diego  quick answers: COBRA is triggered by a qualifying event like termination. We (the COBRA vendor) send an election notice to your home email within 14 days of notification; you then have 60 days to elect continuation coverage and pay premiums. Its not automatic coverage  you must elect.
Priya (Benefits): Dependent Care FSA: reimbursements are allowed only for expenses incurred on or after the plan effective date for your election. We cannot reimburse expenses that predate your coverage/election. If your election becomes effective 1st of next month, only expenses from that date forward are eligible.
Maya (People Ops): Parental leave: the standard handbook requires 12 months of service for the full parental benefit, but individual offer letters can and do override in specific cases. If your offer explicitly guarantees earlier eligibility, please DM a copy of the offer and we'll mark your file and coordinate with payroll/benefits so the correct leave code applies.
Lina (Payroll): For direct deposit: if Workday shows pending it usually missed the payroll cutoff. Confirm routing/account numbers under Workday > Pay > Direct deposit. If you entered them after cutoff it will apply to the next paycheck. If you need urgent help we can do a one-time manual ACH if you email payroll@redwood.example with subject "Manual ACH Request - Diego" before EOD.
Jamal (Finance): FYI on COBRA operationally: our vendor is BenefitGuard (cobra@vendor.example). We notify them on term events and they produce the mailer. Also, reimbursements via FSA are paid as non-payroll reimbursements and go out weekly once claims are approved.
