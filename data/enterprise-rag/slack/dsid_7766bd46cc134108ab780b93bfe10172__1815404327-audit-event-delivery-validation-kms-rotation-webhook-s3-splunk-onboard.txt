samir: For webhook signature verification sample (curl):

```bash
payload='{"audit_schema_version":"v2","event_id":"e1"}'
ts=$(date -u +%s)
sig=$(echo -n "$ts.$payload" | openssl dgst -sha256 -hmac "$CUSTOMER_SECRET" -binary | base64)

curl -X POST https://customer-webhook/ingest -H "Content-Type: application/json" -H "X-Redwood-Timestamp: $ts" -H "X-Redwood-Signature: sha256=$sig" -d "$payload"
```
