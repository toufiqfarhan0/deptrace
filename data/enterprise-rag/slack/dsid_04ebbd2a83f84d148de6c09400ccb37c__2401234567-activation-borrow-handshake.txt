marta: micro-bench on A10, model=gpt-small, seq=512, concurrency=8.

jamal: +2% tail is annoying. any clue where the tail comes from?

marta: flamegraphs point to a few extra mutex+reclaim stalls in slab allocator when a borrowed buffer gets returned late and forces a compact path. Not huge but shows at high concurrency.

carmen: can you paste the runtime flag and repro commands?

marta: sure. config toggle we used:

```\n# runtime.conf\nallocator.enable_buffer_borrowing = true\nallocator.borrow_max_lifetime_ms = 50\nallocator.borrow_prefer_slab = true\n```

repro: `./bench --model gpt-small --seq 512 --concurrency 8 --iters 200 --enable-borrow`

li: borrow_max_lifetime looks suspicious. if a cached KV lookup or kernel swap stalls for >50ms you get pinned buffers. maybe make it adaptive based on observed kernel latency.
