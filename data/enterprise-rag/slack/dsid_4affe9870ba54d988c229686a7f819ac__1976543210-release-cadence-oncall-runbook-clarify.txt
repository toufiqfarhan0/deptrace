elaine_cto: canaries are our standard for feature rollouts. typical canary window 12-48h; higher-risk changes get staged cohorts. promote after the agreed monitoring window stays stable.

sam_ops_lead: who answers pages?

sre_oncall_bot: Rule: the person listed as canary_owner in the rollout PR is the primary responder for the canary window. They must post a kickoff in #eng-oncall and remain reachable for the window. If SLA-tagged routes are touched, cc SRE lead before promoting >20% traffic.

lucy_eng: quality gates: CI smoke and core regression subset green, p95 latency <= 1.2x baseline, service error delta < 0.1% absolute, and no customer-facing regressions. for model/inference changes include eval-run pass & no quality drift on key prompts.
