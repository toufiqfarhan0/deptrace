Jun: fwiw gRPC streaming is much nicer for token streaming; REST would need chunked encoding and it's finicky. also quotas: do we count per-stream or per-response? semantics matter for billing.

Alex: gateway pattern tradeoff — single central gateway that transcodes vs small sidecars doing proto speak internally. what do we prefer?
Priya: central gateway simplifies rollout & A/B, but it's a single throttling/failure blast radius and it can hide per-service quota metrics. sidecars keep service-local quota decisions, but more ops.
Sam: hybrid maybe? edge gateway transcodes for public clients; internal cluster traffic stays gRPC. router policy decides based on client type. need to model quota_key mapping in route config.
