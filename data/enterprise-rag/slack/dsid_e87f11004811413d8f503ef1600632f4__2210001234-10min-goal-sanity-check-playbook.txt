maya (People Ops): micro playbook for a 10-minute goal sanity check before you finalize OKRs. put this in your kickoff thread or a 1:1 quick slot.

maya (People Ops): TL;DR: confirm scope, set 3 measurable signals, pick one immediate deliverable, and list a single help ask.

maya (People Ops): ```10-min Goal sanity check script
0:00-0:90s - restate the objective in one sentence (assume another person can't read history)
1:30-3:00m - confirm owner and top stakeholder(s)
3:00-6:00m - agree 3 signals (metric or artifact) that mean progress
6:00-8:00m - decide the very next deliverable (owner + due date)
8:00-10:00m - one explicit ask of manager/team + sync cadence
```

maya (People Ops): quick signal examples you can paste: 
• "KR signal: weekly API latency 95p <= 120ms (baseline 180ms)"
• "Artifact signal: shipping A/B experiment + dashboard link"
• "Customer signal: 5 CS quotes mentioning performance uplift"
