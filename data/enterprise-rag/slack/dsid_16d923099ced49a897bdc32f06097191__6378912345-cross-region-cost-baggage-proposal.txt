sam: Hmm. Clever, but size + tampering are my immediate concerns. Adding another header to all requests impacts caches and some infra. Also what happens on retries and redirects? :thinking:

maria: Agree on tampering. We should HMAC sign + version. Rotation is annoying but doable (key ids). Alternative: opaque token that maps to a small record in a short-lived sidecar kv. That keeps header tiny.

alex: Tradeoffs to call out: early enrichment (ingest-time) helps query latency and reduces analytic joins, but increases ingest cardinality and storage delta. Late-binding (query-time) keeps cold storage smaller but pushes load to realtime join systems and makes SLAs for cost reports harder.
