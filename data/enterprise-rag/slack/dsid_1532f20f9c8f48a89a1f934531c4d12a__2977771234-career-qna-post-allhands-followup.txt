Emma P.: Hey team — following up from the all-hands Q&A. Im opening this thread for any remaining questions about reviews, promotions, comp timing, and benefits. Ill post quick answers here and link docs.

Jordan L.: Thanks Emma. Quick one: if a manager submits a promotion packet this week but calibration happens next month, when is the official effective date?

Emma P.: Good question. Promotions approved during the current cycle take effect at the start of the next pay period after approval (so youll see it on that paycheck). If calibration delays approval beyond the cycle, the effective date will be the first pay period after final approval.

Kai N.: Are managers expected to collect examples of work for calibration or will People Ops normalize without docs? Some of our managers are unsure what level of evidence to bring.

Emma P.: Managers should bring concise evidence. Were asking for 24 bullets per candidate: recent impact, scope change, leadership examples (mentoring/cross-team ownership), and any measurable outcomes. Well provide a one-page template by EOD tomorrow.

Priya T.: Is there a standard rubric for cross-functional influence vs. project delivery? Wont those sometimes overlap and managers get confused.

Emma P.: Yes — rubric separates scope (what you own) from influence (who else you drive outcomes for). Well include mapping examples in the template (e.g., owning a service = scope; leading a cross-team initiative = influence).

Noah G.: Out-of-cycle comp increases: what constitutes market urgency? Hiring team asked for guidance to triage cases.

Emma P.: Market urgency is typically an active counteroffer, inability to fill a critical role, or a retention risk backed by evidence. Requests need manager + director justification and go through a retention fund. Expect ~7 business days for review unless marked urgent with supporting market data.

Jordan L.: Clarify: if a comp correction is needed due to payroll error, is the adjustment retroactive?

Emma P.: Payroll errors are corrected retroactively where possible. For changes due to performance/level decisions, retroactive pay is not the standard. Well handle exceptions case-by-case.

Kai N.: Are comp bands visible to ICs or just managers/comp partners? Weve seen questions about transparency.

Emma P.: At launch bands are visible to managers and recruiting; were evaluating options for broader visibility and will share the decision and rationale soon.
