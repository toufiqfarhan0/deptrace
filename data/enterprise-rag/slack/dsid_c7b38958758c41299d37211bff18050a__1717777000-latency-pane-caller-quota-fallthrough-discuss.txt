Dana Park: the console query today looks like: ```sql
SELECT quantile(0.5) as p50, quantile(0.95) as p95, quantile(0.99) as p99
FROM latency_metrics
WHERE route = '{{route_label}}' AND environment = '{{env}}'
GROUP BY caller, model_variant
```
This assumes route_label is present. if headers are lost we end up with many samples attributed only to fallback-model-v2 with caller='unknown'.
