maya: Good — need to agree on circuit-breaker semantics. Two flavors: protective CB (trips to protect infra) vs customer-facing CB (degrades to cheaper variant). How encode both?

sam: Two-tier CB -> infra_cb (node/region availability) and variant_cb (per-customer, per-model). Policy DSL example:

```yaml
policy_id: slo-weighted-1
tiers:
  - name: enterprise
    target_latency_ms: 120
    target_availability_pct: 99.95
    base_priority: 100
    cb_thresholds:
      infra_cb: {error_rate_pct: 1.0, p99_ms: 1000}
      variant_cb: {error_rate_pct: 0.5, p99_ms: 300}
    fallback_chain:
      - model: redwood-7b-q4
      - model: redwood-7b-q4f
      - model: redwood-7b-int8
```

sam: The idea is infra_cb trips region-wide routing to fallback region/model; variant_cb only affects customer's assignment to variant.
