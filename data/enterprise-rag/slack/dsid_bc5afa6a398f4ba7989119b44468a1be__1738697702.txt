Michael Nguyen: so the failing field is the forward target URL. got it.

Hanae Suzuki: minimum sensitive actions:

- change destination type (splunk/sentinel/generic webhook)
- change destination URL/host

- change TLS verify / CA bundle / client cert (mTLS)
- change batching/retry settings if they affect delivery guarantees materially
