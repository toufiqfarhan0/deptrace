```yaml
runtime:
  batching:

    max_batch_size: 48
    max_wait_ms: 30
    prefer_latency: true
  kv_cache:
    enabled: true
    prefix_ttl_ms: 120000
  kernels:
    use_opt_kernels: true
    fallback_variant: safe-v1
```
We intentionally bias toward lower batch size to protect p99.
