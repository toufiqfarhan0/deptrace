kyle: that matches what we saw in earlier experiments: reducing max_split_size_mb forces allocator to reuse smaller free slots instead of carving out huge chunks that then leave unusable holes. Tradeoff: more allocations -> higher kernel-selection overheads and slight p99 cost.

maria: curious about interplay with batching heuristics. Are batcher windows causing sudden spikes in resident set that trigger large contiguous allocations?

aj: yes — batching heuristic groups many long sequences into same micro-batch. If KV cache warmup happens simultaneously (many prefixes cached), per-request KV pages are pinned leading to many medium-sized allocations. Combined with a large max_split_size_mb you can quickly fragment address space.

kyle: KV cache mitigation idea: cap per-worker KV cache to 8GB and implement LRU eviction for cold prefixes during scaling-up windows. That keeps resident from jumping. We can also backpressure batcher when combined resident > threshold.
