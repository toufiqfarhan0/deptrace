niko: sounds right. root cause: fallback selector used a single binary "is_over_capacity" flag which ignored fairness weights and per-tenant backoff policy. when the frontdoor tripped, selector picked cheapest model for all.

marco: proposal: introduce a fallback hierarchy + per-tenant grace curve. hierarchy: no_fallback -> slow_path (batching + keep-kv) -> smaller_model -> tiny_model. pick the first allowed level that preserves fairness share.

anya: important: fairness must be preserved across fallback levels. if tenant A has weight 1 and B has weight 0.5, A should keep ~2x tokens across fallbacks. so selector needs to consult the active fairshare allocator, not just global capacity.

rhea: decision sketch:
- compute per-tenant effective_budget = base_quota * fairshare_weight * grace_multiplier(t)
- grace_multiplier(t) decays from 1.2 -> 0.6 over 10m for transient spikes
- fallback chosen only if effective_budget < request_cost AND host_health_bad
- host_health_bad = p99_latency > 900ms OR queue_depth_pct > 70
- reserved_tier: skip tiny_model fallback; alert+contact instead

marco: about grace_curve: i prefer an exponential backoff on multiplier so short bursts recover fast. example: multiplier(t) = 1 + 0.5 * exp(-t / 60s). initial headroom 1.5 -> asymptote 1.

niko: we also need circuit hysteresis to avoid flip-flopping. require fallback_min_duration=90s and cooldown=180s before upward promotion.

anya: telemetry to add before enabling tiny_model fallback globally:
- per-tenant: fallback_entries_total (labels: level, reason)
- per-tenant: effective_budget_histogram
- per-host: fallback_prevalence_pct
- alert: sustained_tiny_model_pct > 1%/5m in prod

sam: tests: synthetic scenario generator with mixed-weight tenants, tenant A heavy burst, B steady background. verify A gets softened (slow_path) not immediate tiny_model, and fairness ratio A:B stays near 2:1.
