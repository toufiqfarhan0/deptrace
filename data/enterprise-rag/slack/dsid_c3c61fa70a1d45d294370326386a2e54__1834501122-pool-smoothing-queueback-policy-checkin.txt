Li: Queueing helps absorb bursts, but we already have request spikes that blow past queue memory and cause OOMs. Any queue needs a strict cap + eviction policy.

Anita: Can we hybrid? i.e., short queue (200-500ms) then degrade responses (shorter max tokens) then reject. That preserves UX for a little bit and avoids long waits.
