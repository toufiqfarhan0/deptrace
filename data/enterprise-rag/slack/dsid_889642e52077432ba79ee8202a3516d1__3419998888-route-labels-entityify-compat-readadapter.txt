Marta: yes — we will publish a compatibility view `events_with_labels_v1`:
```sql
create view events_with_labels_v1 as
select e.*, array_agg(l.key || ':' || l.value) as labels
from events e
left join event_labels el on el.event_id = e.id
left join labels l on l.id = el.label_id
group by e.id;
```
