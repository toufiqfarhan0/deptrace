Alice Chen: quick proposal — region-aware pacing for saturated pools. idea: when a region hits a soft threshold (e.g. 75-80%), switch certain routes to a slower per-route pacing rather than global hard throttles. lockstep fallback to cheaper model variants if queue growth persists.
