devin: Can you paste the exact pipeline?
claire: Sure:
```
# embed pipeline
chunks = chunk_text(doc, max_tokens=128, stride=32)
vecs = [model.embed(c) for c in chunks]
vecs = [v / np.linalg.norm(v) for v in vecs]  # per-chunk L2 norm
avg = np.mean(vecs, axis=0)
final = avg / np.linalg.norm(avg)
index.add(final)
```
