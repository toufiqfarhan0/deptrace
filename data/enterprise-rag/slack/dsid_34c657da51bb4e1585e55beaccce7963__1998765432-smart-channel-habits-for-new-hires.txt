tom: if the thread is quiet, wait ~30-45 minutes during working hours, then bump with a short reply ("bumping, still blocked") instead of a new root message. Only @channel for real-team-wide emergencies. Reactions for ack (✅) > separate "thanks" posts.

infra-bot: Bot tip: for build/deploy noise, create a private channel and `/invite @releases` there, then run `@releases subscribe repo=org/repo filter=team:<your-team>` to limit alerts. Use `@infra-bot mute channel=#random` to silence bot pings in that channel.

maya: great. any quick conventions for code/log snippets in channels?

ben: paste short snippets in fenced blocks and include the command you ran + one-line expected vs actual. Example:

Include env info if relevant. If creds or stack traces have secrets, DM IT or redact before posting.

maya: last one — how do i opt into release and infra alerts without the whole company noise?

infra-bot: In #release-notifs run: `@releases subscribe repo=org/repo team=<team-slug> severity>=warning`. For infra alerts in #eng-infra: `@infra-bot subscribe team=<team-slug> level>=error`. Use `@releases prefs` or `@infra-bot list` to review subscriptions. Critical P0s will still publish regardless of quiet filters.

onboard-bot: Onboarding tip: To get 30/60/90 reminders, run `@onboard-bot remind cadence=30d start=next_monday` in your #new-hires thread or DM the bot. People Ops will follow up in-thread within 24 hours.
