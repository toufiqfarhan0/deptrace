marco-sre: A few things: 1) Priority ordering is reversed compared to intended. 2) We expect numeric weights (70, 30) not strings; our policy-merger sorts by weight when merging, but if weights are strings it ends up comparing lexicographically which can invert order. 3) sticky_ttl is expressed in minutes in the new console UI, but our runtime interpreted sticky_ttl as seconds; 5 -> 5s causing sticky routing to expire immediately and let regular weight logic decide.
