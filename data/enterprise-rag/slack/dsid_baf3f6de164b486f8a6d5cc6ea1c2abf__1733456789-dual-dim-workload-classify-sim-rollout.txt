sophia: proposal: expand classifier from [prompt_len] -> [prompt_len, decode_rate_bucket]. decode_rate_bucket computed from short warm-window (first 8 tokens decode latency). then admission policy uses token_budget_target = base_budget * (1 + alpha*(1 - kv_hit_rate)). so if kv_hit low, allow higher token_budget to form larger batches.
