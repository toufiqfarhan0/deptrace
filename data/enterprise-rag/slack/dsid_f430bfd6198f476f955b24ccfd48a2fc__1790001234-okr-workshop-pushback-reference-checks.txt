ting (Product): any tiny rules-of-thumb for setting 1-2 KRs for ICs vs teams?

kelly (People Ops): RULES-OF-THUMB:
- IC goal: 1 Objective + 1-2 KRs tied to a measurable outcome the person can own.
- Team goal: 1 Objective + 2-3 KRs that require cross-functional work.
- Keep quarterly targets ambitious but achievable (stretch ~15-30%).
