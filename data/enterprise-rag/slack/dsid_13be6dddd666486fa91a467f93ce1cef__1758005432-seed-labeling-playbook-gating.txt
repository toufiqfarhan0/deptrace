Priya K: SQL draft below — very small: 
```sql
-- seed label for cohort based on onboarding event stream
INSERT INTO cs_labels.seed_labels (customer_id, label, source, stamped_at)
SELECT customer_id, 'onboard_v2', 'onboard_events', CURRENT_TIMESTAMP()
FROM analytics.onboarding_events
WHERE cohort_tag = '2026-03-onboard'
  AND onboarding_step = 'final_check'
  AND call_completed = TRUE
  AND customer_id NOT IN (SELECT customer_id FROM cs_labels.seed_labels WHERE label = 'onboard_v2');
```
Priya K: then the playbook service would check cs_labels.seed_labels before executing outbound flows. if missing, it adds a queue and skips immediate touchpoints.
