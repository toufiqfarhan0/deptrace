priya_ml: Preliminary local repro: when calibration times out we observe a mix of kernels. The int8 fast matmul isn't always chosen — the fallback generic fp16 matmul gets selected for longer sequence lengths and ends up costing more because it flushes and re-allocs KV caches. I saw p95 jump from ~210ms to ~860ms in my run when calibration completed partially. When forcing full calibration, int8 path is consistently faster (p95 ~230ms).

alex_runtime: Good find. Two quick mitigations: A) increase calibration timeout for rollout (QUANT_CALIBRATION_TIMEOUT=120) and ensure the calibration job runs off the hot path (background job with warmup). B) Add a runtime safeguard: if calibration_status!=complete then prefer fp16 for that model instance instead of mixing kernels (we should avoid mixed kernels).
