ben: sounds like a classic ownership bug introduced when we switched from single-threaded buffer reuse to pooled mutable buffers for perf. The pool code reused a Vec<u8> and we handed out slices without cloning. When the async validator spawned, it mutated the pool.

hugo: immediate mitigation: set env var `REDWOOD_RUST_NO_BUFFER_POOL=1` in the starter repo; that forces per-request allocations and stops the crash. I pushed a tiny starter patch with the env var and reproducer. Partner unblocked.
