ben: ok, is it deterministic across seeds and machines?

chao: sounds like head-layout vs packer indexing. did the converter emit any head_map/perm metadata?

ben: runtime assumes contiguous head ordering (0..N-1). if converter left a permuted layout, packer offsets will point at wrong kv slices. combined with non-zero pad_id that shifts our internal indexing, we can index padded memory and get garbage.
