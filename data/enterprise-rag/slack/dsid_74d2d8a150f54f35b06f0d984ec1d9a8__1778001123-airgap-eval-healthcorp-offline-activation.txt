tj_engineer: Implementation details and constraints:
- Bundle layout: redwood-bundle-vX.tar.gz (manifests/ + images/ + checksums/SHA256.SF + license.txt)
- Image loading: use crictl or ctr depending on runtime. Example: `crictl load images.tar` or `ctr image import images.tar`
- Typical sizes for our builds: runtime ~900MB (compressed), medium quantized model ~1.5GB, large ~6GB. Expect total bundle 31GB for core+1 model, 80GB for core+3 models.
- Storage: prefer XFS ext4 on SSD for model mmap performance; NFS for model store is allowed but slower for cold starts.
- Min infra for POC: 2 nodes (1 control-plane + 1 worker) acceptable; for HA prod 3 control + N workers.
- Kernel/runtime: containerd 1.5+ or cri-o 1.20+, kernel >=5.10 recommended.

ken_ops: Upgrades and cadence for airgap installs:

- For air-gapped rolling upgrades we provide a delta bundle option where only changed images are included to reduce transfer size.
