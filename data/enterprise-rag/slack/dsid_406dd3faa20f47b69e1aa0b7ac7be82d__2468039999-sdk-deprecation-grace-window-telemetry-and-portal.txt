maria-partners: For the gating policy, propose: 
- Soft deprecation announcement (public) -> 90-day grace window.
- At day 45 evaluate adoption.
- If >= 80% of "active" installs (active = at least 1 heartbeat in last 30 days) have upgraded, schedule hard deprecation 21 days later.
- If <80%, either extend by 30 days or offer a compatibility shim.
This is intentionally conservative for marketplace partners and SIs.

raj-engineering: Slight tweak: make the hard-deprecation countdown configurable per-partner for enterprise accounts (e.g., partners with contracts can request +30 day extension automatically). Implementation: server flags per partner and a boolean `hard_block_enabled` that flips when conditions met.

leanne-product: We'll also need comms assets: staging banner text, partner email template, changelog snippet, and a support playbook for SIs. I can draft the templates and a migration checklist.

toshi-sre: Safety rollback: if after enabling a hard block for a partner group we see >10% increase in 5xx error-rate or a >25% drop in successful request volume for that partner in a 2-hour window, revert block for that partner and alert oncall + partner lead. We'll keep a manual override in the console for three business days after a flip.
