Talia Benson: Heads up for everyone — **Hot-route capacity protection is now GA** for Hosted API + Dedicated. This is the work to prevent a small number of high-traffic / high-SLA routes from dragging the whole fleet when we’re under pressure.

What changed (tldr):
• Tier-aware **rate limits/quotas** at the gateway
• **Priority routing + admission control** so enterprise budgets are preserved under load
• **Graceful degradation** for best-effort traffic (predictable 429s vs fleetwide p99/5xx)
• New overload observability + runbooks so on-call isn’t guessing

Impact so far (first ~1–2w post-launch):
• Fewer fleetwide latency cascades during bursts; enterprise routes stay within SLO more consistently
• Best-effort traffic sees **earlier / more deterministic throttling** during pressure (expected)
• Support now has reason codes to distinguish **quota** vs **overload shedding**
