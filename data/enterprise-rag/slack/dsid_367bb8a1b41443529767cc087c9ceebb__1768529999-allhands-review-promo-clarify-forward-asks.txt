maria: Great, thanks @sam. I'll answer Qs below and drop links. Short answers first, then details if folks want them.
kevin: First from eng managers: how do parental leaves affect review cycles? If someone is on leave during calibration, do we pause their review?
maria: Good Q. We factor qualifying leaves into review decisions. Options: (1) pause review and shift cycle (manager + people ops agree on new window), or (2) proceed with adjusted expectations and annotated calibration notes. We recommend pausing if the leave is >4 weeks during calibration window.
tara: Are paused reviews common? And does pausing delay promo/comp eligibility?
maria: Not common but used. Pausing delays effective date; compensation adjustments apply from the new effective date. We track paused cases in People Ops so nothing falls through.

kevin: Second Q: are calibration meetings recorded or anonymized summaries used? Managers ask about confidentiality.
maria: Calibration is manager-only. We store anonymized notes (no names) for audit. If a discussion needs to be resurrected, People Ops will surface the rationale without exposing private performance commentary.
sam: Third: spot bonuses / recognition awards  can they be added outside normal comp cycles and how are they funded?
maria: Yes, spot bonuses are available year-round for exceptional contributions. Manager submits Spot Bonus request with justification and budget code. Approval is by Comp Manager for amounts >$X. These are separate from base comp/promo decisions.
tara: What about equity refreshes  are those tied to promotions or tenure? Any guidance on timing?
maria: Equity refreshes are discretionary and tied to role, market movement, and retention signals. We do periodic refresh cycles and also consider refreshes with promotions in high-impact cases. Comp Team evaluates case-by-case.
kevin: If someone transfers internally into a new team, do they get an immediate leveling review or wait for next cycle?
maria: Internal transfers trigger a short-form review: manager submits transfer packet with prior role notes + new role expectations. People Ops may approve an immediate rebanding if justified (esp. cross-functional promotions). Otherwise, they enter the next standard cycle.
sam: A manager concern about appeals: can appeals be anonymous or reviewed by an independent panel if there's perceived manager bias?
maria: Appeals are documented and reviewed by People Ops. For potential bias, we can escalate to an independent committee (Comp & People Ops members) and include cross-functional reviewer(s). Anonymity is limited due to context needed, but we minimize exposure and follow privacy best practices.
kevin: Any SLA on appeal resolution? Managers want clear expectations.
maria: Target SLAs: triage within 5 business days, resolution within 30 calendar days for standard appeals. Complex cases may take longer; we'll communicate updates.
kevin: Payroll timing edge cases: if comp change approved after payroll cutoff, when does it hit? People ask about retro pay.
maria: If after cutoff, change applies to next payroll. Retroactive pay is rare; we evaluate if the delay caused material harm and might process an adjustment on a case-by-case basis.
tara: Can managers see anonymized calibration distributions for their org to benchmark decisions?
maria: Yes, we publish aggregated calibration heatmaps to managers (no PII). Those will be on the manager dashboard in Console.

sam: One more: if a manager wants to recommend someone for an out-of-cycle promotion due to critical product need, what is the fastest path?
maria: Submit an expedited promo form flagged "business-critical" with manager justification and supporting customer/impact evidence. People Ops will prioritize and aim for a 10-business-day turnaround for genuine critical cases. Include comp delta and proposed effective date.
kevin: Does "business-critical" promo require additional approval layers?
maria: It may require Product/Revenue owner signoff if tied to customer commitments, and Comp lead approval for sizable comp adjustments. We'll call out required approvers in the intake form.
