yuki: Nice. What pooling + preproc did you use? any special per-dim scaling?
cass: Pooling: mean over token outputs by default, tried CLS and last4-concat as well. For the mixed-bit pipeline we used per-dimension variance bucketing then asymmetric L2 rescale before PQ (helps codebook allocation). Eval command I ran below:

```
./embed_eval \\
  --model verdant-v0.5 \\
  --pooling mean --normalize true \\
  --quant mixed-int --pq-config "m=16,bits=[8,6,4]" --opq 32 \\
  --index hnsw --M 32 --ef_construction 200 \\
  --holdout holdout-30k.jsonl \\
  --metrics recall@1,5,10,neighbor_consistency,silhouette,ari
```
