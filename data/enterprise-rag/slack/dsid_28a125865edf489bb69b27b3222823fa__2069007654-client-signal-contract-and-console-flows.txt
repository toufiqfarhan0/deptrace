maya: here's a compact proposal to discuss (mapping + telemetry):
```
# client_status | http_status | console_badge | telemetry_tags
OK             | 200         | green         | routing=primary;model=prod
DEGRADED       | 200 + header | amber        | routing=fallback;fallback_model=<id>
THROTTLED      | 429         | red           | routing=throttle;quota_bucket=<id>
ERROR          | 500         | grey          | routing=error;internal_code=<code>
```
notes: DEGRADED uses 200 to avoid breaking clients but includes `X-Redwood-Mode: degraded` and `X-Redwood-Fallback: <model-id>` headers.
