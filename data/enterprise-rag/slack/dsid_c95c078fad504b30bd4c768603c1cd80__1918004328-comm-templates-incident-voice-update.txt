Jess (eng-sre): Internal template (short):
```
[Severity] Incident: {{incident_id}}
Reported at: {{start_time}}
Owner: {{oncall_name}} / {{escalation}}
Summary: one-sentence summary of what is happening
Current impact: who/what is affected (services, regions, % requests / example customers)
Mitigation in progress: bullet list of actions
Next update: ETA or timeboxed follow-up
Action items: who is doing what (names + ETA)
Links: runbooks, traces, deploys, status page
```

Jess (eng-sre): Customer-facing template (short):
```
We're aware of an issue affecting {{service_name}} since {{start_time}}. Some requests may experience increased latency or errors. Our team is actively investigating and working on a fix.
We will provide updates by {{next_update_time}}.
If you see degraded behavior, please reply to this message or contact support at {{support_link}}.
```
