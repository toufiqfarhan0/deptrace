Olivia: This is exactly what we saw in design reviews. UX work generally has lower absolute reach but high impact on retention/CSAT. If we leave Reach raw, we systematically deprioritize UX.

Maya: Right. Proposed heuristic (2-step):
1) Clamp Reach at the 90th percentile to reduce outliers.
2) Convert clamped Reach into percentile rank 0..1 within the quarter, then scale to 1..10.
3) Keep Impact on 1..10 but apply a small multiplier for customer-facing signals (x1.2) if a ticket references direct customer ROI.
4) Compute Score = (Reach_scaled * Impact * Confidence) / Effort.
Rationale: keeps RICE shape but prevents single huge Reach from dominating.

Ben: I prototyped the formula. Example using the sample above after clamp/percentile:
```
# pseudo results after clamp+percentile -> Reach_scaled
projA Reach_scaled=10 Impact=8 C=0.8 Effort=20 => Score=(10*8*0.8)/20=3.2
projB Reach_scaled=2  Impact=9 C=0.9 Effort=5  => Score=(2*9*0.9)/5=3.24
projC Reach_scaled=8  Impact=6 C=0.6 Effort=10 => Score=(8*6*0.6)/10=2.88
projD Reach_scaled=1  Impact=7 C=0.7 Effort=4  => Score=(1*7*0.7)/4=1.225
```
Note projB (small reach but very high impact/confidence/low effort) surfaces higher than projA now.
