ana: what is hybrid-prefetch-cache doing differently vs previous prefix-cache?

samir: it combines lightweight probabilistic prefetch (learned from recent access patterns) with a small LRU faststore. we only prefetch when the model's next-token entropy is low and when concurrent eligibility > 2. This reduces unnecessary KV materialization for low-variance conversational prefixes.

samir: config snippet:
```
cache.mode = "hybrid-prefetch"
cache.prefetch_prob_threshold = 0.7
cache.faststore_mb = 96
cache.min_concurrent_for_prefetch = 2
```
