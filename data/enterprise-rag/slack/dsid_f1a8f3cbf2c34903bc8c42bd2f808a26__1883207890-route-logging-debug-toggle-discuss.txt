Riley: Telemetry cost query example for console cost preview (pseudo-SQL):

```sql
SELECT route, count(*) as samples, sum(token_count) as tokens, 
       sum(token_count)*0.0001 as est_cost
FROM sampled_logs
WHERE org_id = 'ORG_X' AND ts >= now() - interval '30 days'
GROUP BY route
```
