Need to reorder: permissions -> secret-visibility -> actions. Also add visible focus ring: `outline: 2px solid var(--focus)` for keyboard users.

Let's add: "The old key is revoked immediately. You have 14 days to re-enable via soft-delete." and provide a link to rollback docs.
