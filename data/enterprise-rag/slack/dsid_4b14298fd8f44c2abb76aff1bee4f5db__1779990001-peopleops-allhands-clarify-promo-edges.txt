Alex (IC): and for ICs who switch teams at the end of the cycle — does the receiving manager get to advocate for a late evidence packet?

Natalie (Finance): payroll clarification: retroactive increases that are approved in a given month take effect the next payroll run. Example: change approved 4/4 -> processed in payroll cutoff on 4/15 -> reflected in 4/30 paycheck. If you need faster processing for comp corrections, ping payroll ops with the ticket number.
Sam (Eng Manager): thanks — what's the cutoff to guarantee next-cycle processing?
Natalie (Finance): cutoff is EOD on the 10th of the month for the mid-month run, EOD on the 25th for month-end. If approval lands after cutoff it goes to the next cycle. We'll publish a calendar.
Alex (IC): about raises + parental leave — is someone who is on active parental leave during calibration eligible for merit? Heard both yes/no.
Meera (People Ops): eligible. We treat leaves as protected time — managers should include the full-period contribution summary. If work was paused due to leave, manager can note that but still recommend based on demonstrated impact. We do not disqualify based on leave.
Alex (IC): appreciate that. Also what's the process for an appeal if an IC thinks calibration missed a key project?
Meera (People Ops): appeals are for calibration/process errors or missed evidence (not subjective disagreements). Steps: 1) Manager submits a calibration correction form within 7 days of notification. 2) People Ops reviews with Finance+Director. 3) Outcome within 10 business days. Use this template: ```Subject: Calibration Correction - [Name] - [Cycle]
