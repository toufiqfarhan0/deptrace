Also top-line `po_number` and `invoice_number`. Keep labels: 'Invoiced To' and 'Legal Entity / VAT'. (1753021950)
anya: A couple of formatting bits: 1) Address needs to be multiline and preserve line breaks (customers have long names + department lines). 2) Support non-latin chars (UTF-8). 3) Line-items: we should show qty, unit price, line subtotal, and `tax_amount` per line, plus an aggregated tax breakdown. Some EU customers explicitly ask for tax-by-country. (1753022010)
