clara_peopleops: TL;DR pls?

People Ops: TL;DR  for the next 6 months employees may swap up to 4 PTO days for up to 8 flexible remote days (2:1 ratio). Swapped remote days must be used within the same quarter and require a 48h manager notice. This is meant for short-term caregiving, phased returns, or timezone transitions.

tom_engineering: Is manager approval needed? Any impact on accruals?

People Ops: Manager acknowledgment required (not a blocking approval) to confirm coverage; HR flags the swap in payroll. Accruals are adjusted so the PTO bank reduces immediately; the remote days are tracked as a non-accrual benefit and won't affect future balances.

mira_manager: What about international remote days? Any travel/travel-policy overlap?

People Ops: International remote work follows the existing Remote Work Abroad rules; swapping doesn't bypass tax or visa requirements. If remoteing from another country for >14 days per quarter, notify People Ops 10 business days before travel. See the policy page for region notes.
