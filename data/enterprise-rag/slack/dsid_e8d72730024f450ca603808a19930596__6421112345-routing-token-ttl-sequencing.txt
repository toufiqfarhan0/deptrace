Alex M: quick idea — we keep seeing unattributed quota spikes when short-lived SA tokens are exchanged across projects. what if we route tokens with TTL <= 5m to a dedicated fast-path pool with strict attribution rules?
Priya K: curious — do you mean route based on token TTL at the auth-proxy layer? also what would the strict attribution rules be?

Tom B: pros: avoids noisy billing spikes and makes quota sane for exchange-heavy flows. cons: might fragment capacity and make SLO tuning harder.
