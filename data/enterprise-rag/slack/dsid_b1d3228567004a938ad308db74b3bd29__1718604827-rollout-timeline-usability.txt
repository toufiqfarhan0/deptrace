alex (Design): Timeline looks useful but feels noisy at full expansion. sam (FE): Agree on collapsing. Implementation Q: Should diffs be lazy-loaded? could be heavy. Also the canary percentage slider must be keyboard accessible — arrow keys adjust 1%, Shift+arrow 5%. Need clear aria-labels and live region announcing percentage changes.

lucy (Accessibility): A few a11y nits: timeline tick labels currently low contrast — increase contrast to meet 4.5:1 for body text. Tooltip triggers must be focusable (not just hover). Modals must trap focus and return focus to trigger. Icons need alt text or aria-labels.

jordan (Platform): For A/B tests, show stable variant IDs, not just "V1/V2". Suggest format: "v3 • a2f4" (short hash). Also display traffic split in absolute terms as well as percent (e.g., "20% — ~2/10 requests") to help non-technical PMs.

kevin (SRE): Rollback copy must be explicit about in-flight requests. Proposed copy: "Rollback to v2 — active connections will continue; new requests will be routed to v2. The rollback will be recorded in the audit log with user and reason." Also consider a "dry run" toggle for staging rollbacks.

alex (Design): UX question: In the rollback confirmation modal should default focus be on Cancel (safer) or Confirm (faster)? For major rollbacks I recommend requiring typing the commit hash to confirm. For minor config-only rollbacks maybe a checkbox is enough.

mia (PM): I like commit-hash confirmation for major rollbacks. Let's standardize: major rollbacks require typing commit id; minor ones use a checkbox "Preserve traffic percentage". We'll document behavior in the help pill.
