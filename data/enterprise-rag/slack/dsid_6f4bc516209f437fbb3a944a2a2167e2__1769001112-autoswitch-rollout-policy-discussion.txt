Alice Chen: Quick question — for canary rollouts should we let the router auto-switch to fallback variants on observed regressions, or gate to manual approval? Thinking of model variant fallback, region failover, and capacity-tier fallback. Need guardrails and telemetry to avoid oscillation.

Tom Rivera: my two cents: auto fallback OK if we set strong hysteresis + cooldowns. Prefer a tiered strategy: 1) model-variant fallback (safer/less-quantized), 2) regional failover (same model in different AZ/region), 3) capacity degrade (lower batch or degraded instance class). Different steps require different signals.
