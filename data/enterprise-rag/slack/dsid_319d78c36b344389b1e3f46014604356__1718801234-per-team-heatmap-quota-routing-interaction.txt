 nate-analytics: SQL prototype (rough):
```
SELECT team_id, model, floor(amortized_cost*10)/10 AS cost_bucket,
  floor(cache_savings*10)/10 AS cache_bucket, count(*) as reqs,
  sum(tokens) as tokens, avg(latency_p95) as p95
FROM preagg_request_cache
WHERE ts >= now() - interval '7 day'
GROUP BY 1,2,3,4
```
