tobi-eng: quick primer: there are 2 layers to defend against dead connections:
  - TCP-level: SO_KEEPALIVE / kernel tcp_keepalive_* (packets from OS)
  - App-level: SSE comment pings (lines starting with a colon) or `event: ping` messages so clients can detect liveness.
