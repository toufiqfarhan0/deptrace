Ravi Narayan: welcome Maya! :tada: On WSL we usually use linuxbrew for parity with mac. If you installed it, OpenSSL lives under /home/linuxbrew/.linuxbrew/opt/openssl@3 — set these before building: `export LDFLAGS="-L/home/linuxbrew/.linuxbrew/opt/openssl@3/lib"`, `export CPPFLAGS="-I/home/linuxbrew/.linuxbrew/opt/openssl@3/include"`, and `export PKG_CONFIG_PATH="/home/linuxbrew/.linuxbrew/opt/openssl@3/lib/pkgconfig"`. Also ensure zlib1g-dev, libbz2-dev, libreadline-dev, libsqlite3-dev are installed.
