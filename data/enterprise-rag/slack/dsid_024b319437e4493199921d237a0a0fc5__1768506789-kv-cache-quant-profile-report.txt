anika_p: quick update — ran a focused KV-cache quant test comparing fp8 vs int8 on the 7B encoder-decoder we plan to onboard. goal: measure mem footprint, p50/p95 token latency, and small QA accuracy delta. sharing raw notes below.

chao_w: curious — did you test fused fp8 kernels or emulated fp8 via int8 paths?

anika_p: we used fused fp8 kernels on our runtime (same kernels we'll ship to dedicated). int8 used our current quant path (symmetric 8-bit with per-channel scales). note: both use cached kv in quant form with fast dequant on reuse.

anika_p: results summary (single-GPU numbers):
```
config           | kv_mem (GB) | p50_token_ms | p95_token_ms | tokens/s | QA acc delta
fp32 baseline     | 1.28        | 22.4         | 78.1         | 420      | 0.0
int8 kv          | 0.36        | 24.1         | 92.6         | 390      | -0.6%
fp8 kv (e4m3)    | 0.24        | 23.1         | 83.5         | 405      | -0.2%
```

anika_p: takeaways: fp8 saves ~81% vs fp32 on kv memory, int8 saves ~72%; fp8 gives slightly better tokens/s and lower p95 vs int8 because the fused kernel reduces dequant overhead on hot-paths. int8 shows a small p95 tail increase under spikes (seen in prod-like burst tests).

mike_r: did you run multi-GPU shard tests? how does network transfer affect p95 when kv is smaller?

anika_p: yes — on 4xA100 testbed we saw similar trends; smaller kv reduces PCIe/NVLink churn. int8 had a bit more jitter on p95 because our int8 path dequantizes per-request in a non-fused path when a cache miss occurs.

chao_w: what about quality on longer generative traces (story/assistant) — any sign of coherency drops?

anika_p: ran 500 long-form completions (avg 120 tokens). human-fluency score delta: int8 -0.9 pts (out of 100), fp8 -0.3 pts. automated perplexity-based shifts were consistent with the QA slice.

ben_ml: interesting. Are there specific tokens / attention heads where error concentrates? could be candidate for hybrid (fp8 for kv, int8 for weights) or selective higher-precision for certain layers.

anika_p: rough layerwise probing: errors are concentrated in late-layer attention K/V scales for int8; fp8 preserves dynamic range better there. Hybrid idea seems promising — also considering retaining fp32 for last 2 layers' kv.
