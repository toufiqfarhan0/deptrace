raj: couple thoughts: (1) calibrate_min_batches=8 might be low for stable scaling across L40S vs H100 — calibration variance can change scaling factors and cause numeric instability during warmup. (2) kernel_selector:auto sometimes picks a slower but more numerically stable kernel for later tokens; but first-token path may prefer a universal kernel causing the observed fallback.

sasha: we've seen similar on other quant modes — calibration noise -> bad per-layer scaling -> non-deterministic GEMM choose. Could try increasing calibrate_min_batches to 64 and use per-device calibration seeds.

olivia: also the KV async_writeback is probably serializing small writes when the fused kernel can't handle the layout. Try switching to kv_cache_mode: batched_writeback or local_cache for L40S nodes.

maya: ran a quick experiment: calibrate_min_batches=64 + preferred_kernels pinned to fused_attention and fused_gemm removed the fallback spikes on H100, but L40S still had occasional fallback due to a missing fused kernel variant.
