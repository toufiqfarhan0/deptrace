SamW: t-digest merge is approximate. when route A has 90k samples and route B has 900 samples a small tail in A will dominate the merged p95. that can look like a regression even though B changed.

ashok: exactly — product saw the spike and pinged eng. ops noticed it coincides with a 10x weight shift to route A. we should avoid misleading SREs.

Maria: options: (1) show merged pX with a provenance badge (source weights + sample_count), (2) fallback to per-route percentiles if sample imbalance threshold exceeded, (3) show confidence interval around merged percentile (bootstrap-ish).

SamW: (3) is neat but expensive to compute in real-time. (1) is smallest effort. (2) is pragmatic. my vote: (1)+UX note + (2) for big imbalances.

Jordan: technical note — our current query is roughly:
```
SELECT merge_tdigest(tdigest) as td FROM rollups WHERE endpoint='/v1/generate' AND ts BETWEEN ...;
SELECT td.quantile(0.95)
```
We also persist sample_count per route at the rollup granularity.
