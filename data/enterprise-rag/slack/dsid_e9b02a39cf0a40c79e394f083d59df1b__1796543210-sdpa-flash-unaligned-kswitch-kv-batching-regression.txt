sam: Is mask construction allocating per-request? That would explain extra synchronous copies.

maria: I dug in — SDPA fallback path uses a generic mask builder that pads to multiples of 8 and does a memcpy per batch. For head_dim % 8 != 0 we end up allocating aligned buffers and copying bitmasks into them. We should cache the padded-mask per sequence-length + batch-shape and reuse it.

cheng: There's also a KV-cache alignment problem: when we pad KV to 16 for Triton kernels we sometimes duplicate KV tensors across slices which breaks our buffer-sharing assumptions and increases memory ops.

alex: Mitigation plan (short-term): force the SDPA variant that preallocates padded masks and reuse KV buffers. Export envs: RUNTIME_FORCE_SDPA=1 PREALLOC_KV=1. Also throttle batcher so it avoids shapes that hit the fallback often.
